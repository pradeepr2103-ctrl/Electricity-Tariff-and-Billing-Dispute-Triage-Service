KARNATAKA ELECTRICITY SUPPLY CODE 2004
(AS AMENDED UPTO 2022)

Government of Karnataka
Energy Department

Issued under Section 50 of the Electricity Act, 2003
(Central Act 36 of 2003)

This Supply Code governs the terms and conditions for supply of electricity by
Distribution Licensees to consumers in Karnataka.

================================================================================
CHAPTER 1: PRELIMINARY
================================================================================

Regulation 1: Short Title and Commencement
1.1 These regulations may be called the "Karnataka Electricity Supply Code, 2004" and
    the amendments thereto shall be referred to by the year of amendment.
1.2 These regulations shall come into force from the date of their publication in the
    Karnataka Gazette.

Regulation 2: Definitions
2.1 "Act" means the Electricity Act, 2003 (Central Act 36 of 2003).
2.2 "Applicant" means any person applying for electricity supply.
2.3 "Connected Load" means the aggregate wattage of all electrical appliances,
    apparatus or motor rating that may be connected at any time to the supply system.
2.4 "Consumer" means any person who is supplied with electricity by a Distribution Licensee.
2.5 "Distribution Licensee" means the licensee authorised to distribute electricity in a
    specified area of supply.
2.6 "Metering Equipment" means the meter, instrument transformers, metering cubicle,
    meter seals, and associated wiring installed at the consumer premises.
2.7 "Premises" means any land or building or part of a building where electricity supply
    is provided to a consumer.
2.8 "Sanctioned Load" means the connected load approved by the Distribution Licensee
    for supply to a consumer.
2.9 "Service Connection" means all works executed for the purpose of providing a supply
    of electricity up to and including the main switch at the consumer premises.

================================================================================
CHAPTER 2: APPLICATION FOR SUPPLY
================================================================================

Regulation 3: Application for New Connection
3.1 Any person requiring a new electricity supply shall submit an application in the
    prescribed form to the Distribution Licensee.
3.2 The application shall include:
    (a) Nature and purpose of use (residential, commercial, industrial, agricultural)
    (b) Estimated connected load
    (c) Category of supply required (LT/HT)
    (d) Details of premises ownership/tenancy
    (e) Any mixed or additional use of premises

3.3 Wrong Statement in Application
    If a consumer obtains supply by making a false statement or misrepresentation
    regarding the nature or purpose of use, the Distribution Licensee may:
    (a) Revise all bills from the date of connection (or 12 months prior) to correct category
    (b) Levy an additional charge of 15% on the differential amount as penalty
    (c) Disconnect supply pending regularization

Regulation 4: Type of Supply
4.1 Low Tension (LT) Supply: Up to 11 kV, for connected loads generally up to 75 kW
4.2 High Tension (HT) Supply: 11 kV and above, for connected loads above 75 kW
4.3 The Distribution Licensee reserves the right to provide HT supply for loads
    below 75 kW where LT supply is not feasible.

Regulation 5: Security Deposit
5.1 The Distribution Licensee shall collect security deposit equivalent to:
    (a) LT Domestic: 2 months estimated billing
    (b) LT Non-Domestic/Commercial: 3 months estimated billing
    (c) LT Industrial: 3 months estimated billing

================================================================================
CHAPTER 3: METERING
================================================================================

Regulation 6: Metering Obligations
6.1 Every consumer shall be provided with a meter to record consumption.
6.2 The meter shall be installed at or near the point of supply.
6.3 The Distribution Licensee shall own and maintain the meter.

Regulation 7: Meter Installation Standards
7.1 Meters shall conform to the Bureau of Indian Standards specifications:
    - Single Phase Meters: IS 13779
    - Three Phase Meters: IS 14697
    - CT/PT operated meters: IS 13010
7.2 All meters shall have accuracy class 1.0 or better.
7.3 Tamper-evident features shall be provided on all new meters.

Regulation 8: Meter Reading
8.1 Meters shall be read at intervals of not more than 60 days for LT consumers.
8.2 The Distribution Licensee shall issue a bill within 7 days of meter reading.
8.3 If a meter reader cannot access the premises, an estimated bill shall be issued.
8.4 The actual reading shall be taken at the next opportunity and the account adjusted.

Regulation 9: Faulty Meters and Consumer's Right to Test
9.1 A consumer who has reason to believe that their meter is recording incorrectly
    may apply for meter testing.

Section 9.2 — Application for Meter Testing
The consumer shall:
    (a) Submit a written application to the Distribution Licensee
    (b) Pay the prescribed testing fee (Rs. 200 for single phase, Rs. 500 for three phase)
    The fee is non-refundable if the meter is found accurate.

Section 9.3 — Testing Procedure
    (a) The meter shall be tested within 30 days of application
    (b) Testing shall be done by the Distribution Licensee's authorized personnel
    (c) The consumer or their representative may be present during testing
    (d) A test report shall be issued within 7 days of testing

Section 9.4 — Outcome: Meter Accurate
    If the meter is found accurate (error within ±2% of true value):
    (a) The consumer bears the testing fee
    (b) No revision to previous bills
    (c) The meter continues in service

Section 9.5 — Outcome: Meter Defective
    If the meter is found defective (error exceeding ±2%):
    (a) The meter shall be replaced at no cost to the consumer
    (b) Testing fee shall be refunded
    (c) Bills shall be revised for a period not exceeding 6 billing cycles
    (d) Revision based on average of 12 months' consumption prior to the defect

Section 9.6 — Meter Tampering
    If a meter is found to be tampered:
    (a) Assessment shall be made based on 200% of average consumption
    (b) Penalty equal to 2 times the assessed amount shall be levied
    (c) The Distribution Licensee may file a criminal complaint under Section 135 of the Act

================================================================================
CHAPTER 4: BILLING
================================================================================

Regulation 10: Billing Cycle and Due Date
10.1 Bills shall be issued monthly or bi-monthly as determined by the Distribution Licensee.
10.2 Payment shall be due within 15 days from the date of the bill.
10.3 A late payment surcharge of 1.5% per month (simple interest) shall be levied on
     amounts overdue beyond the due date.

Regulation 11: Estimated Billing
11.1 Where actual meter reading is not possible, an estimated bill shall be issued based on:
     (a) The consumer's average monthly consumption in the previous 12 months; or
     (b) The consumption of a comparable consumer if previous data is unavailable.
11.2 Estimated bills shall be clearly marked "Estimated Bill."
11.3 A final adjustment bill shall be issued after the next actual reading.

Regulation 12: Advance Payment Discount
12.1 A discount of 1% shall be allowed for advance payment received 5 days prior to due date.

================================================================================
CHAPTER 5: DISCONNECTION AND RECONNECTION
================================================================================

Regulation 13: Disconnection of Supply
13.1 The Distribution Licensee may disconnect supply after giving due notice for:
     (a) Non-payment of dues beyond the due date (notice of 15 days)
     (b) Theft or unauthorized use of electricity
     (c) Dangerous or defective installation
     (d) Violation of supply conditions

Clause 13.2 — Notice Requirements
Before disconnecting for non-payment:
     (a) Written notice of at least 15 days shall be given
     (b) Notice shall specify the amount due and date of disconnection
     (c) Notice shall be served personally or by registered post

Clause 13.3 — Reconnection
After disconnection, supply shall be restored within 24 hours of:
     (a) Payment of all outstanding dues; AND
     (b) Payment of reconnection charges (Rs. 50 for LT, Rs. 200 for HT)

================================================================================
CHAPTER 6: CONSUMER RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS
================================================================================

Regulation 14: Consumer Obligations
14.1 The consumer shall:
     (a) Use electricity only for the purpose for which the supply is sanctioned
     (b) Notify the Distribution Licensee within 30 days of any change in the nature or
         purpose of use of the premises
     (c) Not tamper with or alter the meter or seals
     (d) Allow Distribution Licensee personnel access for meter reading and inspection
     (e) Not connect load exceeding the sanctioned connected load

14.2 Change in Use of Premises
A consumer who changes the use of their premises:
     (a) From domestic to commercial: must apply for re-categorization within 30 days
     (b) From commercial to domestic: may apply for re-categorization with documentary evidence
     (c) Retrospective billing from date of change (or 12 months, whichever is less) applies
         if change is detected without prior notification

14.3 Home-Based Occupation
A consumer who conducts a home-based occupation shall:
     (a) Notify the Distribution Licensee of the nature of occupation
     (b) Maintain conditions prescribed under Schedule 8 of the KERC Tariff Order
     (c) The Distribution Licensee will assess whether LT-1 or LT-2 categorization applies

Regulation 15: Consumer Rights
15.1 Every consumer has the right to:
     (a) Receive electricity supply of prescribed quality and reliability
     (b) Be billed correctly under the applicable tariff category
     (c) Have their meter tested
     (d) Dispute any bill and receive a written response within 30 days
     (e) Approach the Consumer Grievance Redressal Forum (CGRF)
     (f) Receive compensation for prolonged supply interruptions (as per KERC standards)

Regulation 16: Grievance Redressal
16.1 Consumer Grievance Redressal Forum (CGRF)
     Each Distribution Licensee shall establish CGRF at Division/Circle level.
     Any aggrieved consumer may file a complaint with the CGRF.
     
Section 16.2 — CGRF Jurisdiction
CGRF shall hear complaints regarding:
     (a) Incorrect billing (including tariff category disputes)
     (b) Meter disputes
     (c) Connection-related issues
     (d) Supply quality complaints
     (e) Disconnection disputes

Section 16.3 — CGRF Process
     (a) Consumer files written complaint with CGRF
     (b) CGRF acknowledges within 3 days
     (c) Distribution Licensee submits reply within 15 days
     (d) CGRF passes order within 30 days of receiving reply
     (e) Orders of CGRF are binding on Distribution Licensee

================================================================================
CHAPTER 7: INSPECTION AND ENFORCEMENT
================================================================================

Regulation 17: Right of Inspection
17.1 The Distribution Licensee's authorized officers may inspect any consumer's premises:
     (a) To verify the nature and purpose of use
     (b) To check connected load and metering equipment
     (c) To investigate complaints or meter disputes
     (d) To detect theft or unauthorized use
     (e) At any reasonable time after giving 24 hours' notice (except in emergencies)

Regulation 18: Assessment for Unauthorized Use
18.1 If a consumer is found using electricity for an unauthorized purpose:
     (a) Assessment for the unauthorized period (up to 2 years preceding detection)
     (b) Additional surcharge of 15% on assessed amount
     (c) Option to regularize by applying for the correct category

================================================================================
CHAPTER 8: SPECIAL PROVISIONS FOR SPECIFIC CONSUMER CATEGORIES
================================================================================

Section 19: Agricultural Connections
19.1 Agricultural connections are subject to:
     (a) Verification of agricultural land ownership (or lease)
     (b) Annual declaration by the consumer regarding agricultural use
     (c) Physical inspection if commercial use is suspected

Section 19.2 — Mixed Use of Agricultural Connection
If an agricultural connection is found to be used for non-agricultural purposes:
     (a) The non-agricultural use shall be billed under the applicable commercial category
     (b) A separate connection may be required for non-agricultural use
     (c) Retrospective billing for the non-agricultural use applies

Section 20: Industrial Connections
Section 20.1 — Load Factor Requirements
Industrial consumers must maintain a load factor of at least 35% per month, failing which
a minimum charge equivalent to the contracted load shall apply.

Section 20.2 — Power Factor Clause
Industrial consumers (HT and LT-3) must maintain a power factor of 0.90 or above.
Penalty/incentive provisions apply as per the KERC Tariff Order.

APPENDIX A: TARIFF CATEGORY DETERMINATION MATRIX
================================================================================

Quick Reference Guide for Tariff Category Classification:

| Premises Use          | Connected Load | Applicable Category |
|-----------------------|---------------|---------------------|
| Purely Domestic       | ≤ 10 kW       | LT-1 Domestic       |
| Purely Domestic       | > 10 kW       | LT-1A HEW Domestic  |
| Purely Commercial     | Any           | LT-2 Non-Domestic   |
| Mixed (Dom > 50%)     | Any           | LT-1 + assessment   |
| Mixed (Com > 50%)     | Any           | LT-2 (entire premises)|
| Home-Based Occupation | Any           | LT-1 (if all conditions met) |
| Industrial            | ≤ 75 kW       | LT-3 Industrial     |
| Agricultural          | Any           | LT-4 Agricultural   |

================================================================================
APPENDIX B: DOCUMENTS REQUIRED FOR TARIFF RE-CATEGORIZATION
================================================================================

For re-categorization from Domestic to Commercial (LT-1 to LT-2):
1. Application in prescribed form
2. Nature of business (trade license / registration)
3. Layout plan showing area of commercial use
4. Area measurement certificate
5. Any applicable shop/establishment registration

For home-based occupation (retention in LT-1):
1. Declaration by consumer regarding nature and extent of occupation
2. Self-declaration of number of employees (max 2 from outside)
3. Layout plan (approximate) showing extent of domestic vs. occupation area
4. Undertaking to notify within 30 days if conditions change
