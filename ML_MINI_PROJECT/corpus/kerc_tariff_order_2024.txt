KARNATAKA ELECTRICITY REGULATORY COMMISSION
RETAIL SUPPLY TARIFF ORDER 2024
(ORDER NO. KERC/TARIFF/2024/001)

Dated: 01 January 2024

In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 64 read with Sections 62 and 86 of the
Electricity Act, 2003 (Central Act 36 of 2003), the Karnataka Electricity Regulatory Commission
hereby issues the following Tariff Order applicable to supply of electricity by Distribution
Licensees in Karnataka.

================================================================================
PART I: DEFINITIONS
================================================================================

Section 1 - General Definitions

1.1 "Commission" means the Karnataka Electricity Regulatory Commission (KERC).
1.2 "Distribution Licensee" means BESCOM, HESCOM, MESCOM, GESCOM, or CESC.
1.3 "Connected Load" means the sum of wattage of all electrical appliances/equipment that
    may be connected by a consumer at any given time, expressed in kilowatts (kW).
1.4 "Contracted Demand" means the maximum demand that a consumer can draw, as agreed
    upon in the supply agreement, expressed in kilovolt-amperes (kVA).
1.5 "Domestic Premises" means any premises used solely for residential/dwelling purposes.
1.6 "Non-Domestic Premises" means any premises used for commercial, business, trading,
    professional, or other non-residential purposes.
1.7 "Mixed Use Premises" means any premises used partly for domestic and partly for
    non-domestic purposes simultaneously.
1.8 "Home-Based Occupation" means an occupation or small business conducted by a
    resident occupant of a dwelling from within that dwelling, without altering the residential
    character of the premises, employing not more than two (2) persons from outside the household.

PART II: TARIFF SCHEDULE
================================================================================

CATEGORY LT-1: DOMESTIC (RESIDENTIAL)

Definition:
Electrical energy supplied to premises used for domestic/residential purposes including
houses, flats, apartments, and bungalows where electricity is consumed solely for domestic
purposes such as lighting, fans, air conditioning, domestic appliances (refrigerators,
washing machines, water heaters), and similar domestic uses.

Eligibility:
- Premises must be used exclusively for residential purposes
- Connected load up to 10 kW
- Consumers with connected load above 10 kW will be billed under LT-1A (HEW) category

Tariff Rates (Effective 01 April 2024):
Fixed Charges:
- Up to 4 kW connected load: Rs. 35 per month
- Above 4 kW and up to 10 kW: Rs. 75 per month

Energy Charges (per unit/kWh):
- First 30 units per month: Rs. 4.95 per unit
- Units 31 to 100 per month: Rs. 7.45 per unit
- Units 101 to 200 per month: Rs. 8.05 per unit
- Above 200 units per month: Rs. 9.35 per unit

Minimum Charges: Rs. 50 per month (whichever is higher of fixed charges or minimum)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CATEGORY LT-1A: DOMESTIC HIGH ENERGY CONSUMPTION (HEW)

Definition:
Domestic consumers with connected load exceeding 10 kW.

Eligibility:
- Residential premises with connected load above 10 kW

Tariff Rates (Effective 01 April 2024):
Fixed Charges: Rs. 150 per month
Energy Charges: Rs. 9.35 per unit for all units consumed

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CATEGORY LT-2: NON-DOMESTIC (COMMERCIAL)

Definition:
Electrical energy supplied to premises used for commercial, trading, business establishments,
professional offices, service establishments, shops, showrooms, hotels, restaurants, cinema halls,
educational institutions (private), hospitals (private), and all other non-residential, non-industrial
uses. This includes any premises used primarily for earning profit or conducting business.

Eligibility:
- Any premises used for commercial/business purposes
- Connected load up to 40 kW (above 40 kW falls under HT categories)

Tariff Rates (Effective 01 April 2024):
Fixed Charges:
- Up to 10 kW: Rs. 75 per month
- Above 10 kW and up to 20 kW: Rs. 150 per month
- Above 20 kW and up to 40 kW: Rs. 275 per month

Energy Charges (per unit/kWh):
- All units: Rs. 9.55 per unit (peak hours)
- All units: Rs. 7.55 per unit (off-peak hours)
- All units (flat rate if no TOD meter): Rs. 9.55 per unit

Minimum Charges: Rs. 100 per month

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CATEGORY LT-3: INDUSTRIAL (LOW TENSION)

Definition:
Electrical energy supplied for manufacturing, processing, fabrication, and other
industrial purposes at Low Tension supply.

Eligibility:
- Connected load up to 75 kW
- Must have valid industrial registration/license

Tariff Rates (Effective 01 April 2024):
Fixed Charges: Rs. 100 per kW of connected load per month
Energy Charges: Rs. 8.05 per unit (all units, flat rate)
Minimum Charges: Equivalent to 50 units per kW of connected load per month

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CATEGORY LT-4: AGRICULTURAL PUMPING

Definition:
Electrical energy supplied for agricultural pump sets used for irrigation purposes.

Eligibility:
- Must be used exclusively for agricultural irrigation
- Connection must be on agricultural land
- Pumpsets must be approved capacity

Tariff Rates (Effective 01 April 2024):
- Unmetered supply: Flat rate per HP per annum (category-specific)
- Metered supply: Rs. 1.50 per unit

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CATEGORY LT-5: PUBLIC LIGHTING AND PWD

Definition:
Electrical energy supplied for street lighting, traffic signals, and government public works.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CATEGORY LT-6: TEMPORARY SUPPLY

Definition:
Electrical energy supplied for temporary purposes such as construction, events, exhibitions.

Tariff Rates: 1.5 times the applicable permanent category rate

SCHEDULE 8: MIXED USE PREMISES AND SPECIAL PROVISIONS
================================================================================

General Provision — Mixed Use Premises:

Clause 8.1: Where premises are used for both domestic and non-domestic purposes simultaneously,
the following provisions shall apply to determine the applicable tariff category.

Clause 8.2: Predominant Use Rule — Non-Domestic Area Exceeding 50%
If the non-domestic use area exceeds 50 percent (50%) of the total built-up area of the
premises, the entire premises shall be billed under LT-2 (Non-Domestic) category.
Calculation: Non-domestic area (sq. ft.) / Total built-up area (sq. ft.) × 100 > 50%
→ Apply LT-2 to entire connected load.

Clause 8.3: Home-Based Occupation Provision
Where a portion of a residential premises is used for a small home-based occupation or
business by the occupant-resident, the connection may be retained under LT-1 (Domestic)
category subject to ALL of the following conditions being met:
  (a) The occupation is conducted by the resident occupant of the dwelling;
  (b) Not more than two (2) persons from outside the household are employed;
  (c) The area used for non-domestic purposes does not exceed 50% of total built-up area;
  (d) The nature of occupation does not generate commercial-scale noise, effluent, or hazardous waste;
  (e) The primary character of the premises remains residential;
  (f) No separate commercial signage is installed at the premises.

Examples of qualifying home-based occupations:
- Home-based tailoring/stitching with 1-2 domestic sewing machines
- Private tuition classes with not more than 10 students at a time
- Home-based beauty parlour with 1 operator
- Freelance professionals (consultants, accountants, writers) working from home

Clause 8.4: Disqualification from Home-Based Occupation Provision
A consumer shall NOT be entitled to LT-1 billing if:
  (a) The business employs more than 2 outside persons;
  (b) Industrial machinery or equipment is installed;
  (c) A separate commercial electricity connection has been obtained;
  (d) The non-domestic area exceeds 50% of total built-up area;
  (e) The consumer has previously been notified of misuse and failed to rectify.

Clause 8.5: Site Inspection for Mixed Use
Where there is ambiguity about whether a connection qualifies as home-based occupation,
the Distribution Licensee shall conduct a site inspection within 30 working days of
the dispute being registered.

Clause 8.6: Tariff Revision on Change of Use
If premises are found to be used for non-domestic purposes without prior intimation:
  (a) Retrospective billing under LT-2 from the date of change of use (or 12 months prior,
      whichever is later);
  (b) Additional surcharge of 15% on the differential amount.

Clause 8.7: Consumer's Obligation to Notify
Any consumer who changes the use of their premises from domestic to non-domestic, or
who starts a home-based occupation, must notify the Distribution Licensee within 30 days
of such change.

SCHEDULE 9: METER AND ACCURACY PROVISIONS
================================================================================

Section 9.1: Meter Accuracy Standards
All meters installed by Distribution Licensees shall conform to IS:13779 accuracy class 1.0
for single phase meters and accuracy class 0.5S for three-phase meters.

Clause 9.2: Consumer's Right to Test Meter Accuracy
Any consumer may request meter accuracy testing by:
  (a) Submitting a written application to the Distribution Licensee;
  (b) Depositing the testing fee as specified (non-refundable if meter found accurate);
  (c) The test shall be conducted within 30 days of the application.

Clause 9.3: Meter Found Defective
If the tested meter is found to be defective (reading error > ±2%):
  (a) The meter shall be replaced within 7 days;
  (b) Billing shall be revised for a period not exceeding 6 billing cycles prior to the test;
  (c) Revision shall be based on average consumption of the 12 months prior to the defect.

Clause 9.4: Meter Found Accurate
If the tested meter is found accurate (reading error within ±2%):
  (a) The consumer bears the testing fee;
  (b) No revision to bills shall be made.

Clause 9.5: Meter Burnt or Stop Case
If a meter is found stopped or burnt:
  (a) The Distribution Licensee shall replace it at no cost to the consumer;
  (b) Assessment shall be based on average of previous 6 months or similar consumer load.

================================================================================
SCHEDULE 10: CONNECTED LOAD VIOLATION AND PENALTIES
================================================================================

Clause 10.1: Authorized Connected Load
No consumer shall connect any appliance or equipment that increases the total connected
load beyond the sanctioned connected load without prior permission from the Distribution Licensee.

Clause 10.2: Penalty for Excess Connected Load
If a consumer is found to have connected load in excess of sanctioned load:
  (a) Penalty of Rs. 500 per kW of excess load per month from the date of detection;
  (b) Excess load must be disconnected within 30 days or sanctioned capacity upgraded;
  (c) Distribution Licensee may disconnect supply if violation continues.

Clause 10.3: Regularization of Excess Load
A consumer may apply for regularization of excess load by:
  (a) Filing application with the Distribution Licensee;
  (b) Paying regularization charges as specified;
  (c) Paying arrears of penalty computed from date of violation.

================================================================================
SCHEDULE 11: ARREARS AND DISCONNECTION
================================================================================

Clause 11.1: Payment Due Date
Electricity bills are due for payment within 15 days of the bill date.

Clause 11.2: Late Payment Surcharge
A surcharge of 1.5% per month shall be levied on unpaid amounts after the due date.

Clause 11.3: Disconnection for Non-Payment
Supply may be disconnected if payment is not received within 30 days of due date,
after issuance of a disconnection notice of at least 15 days.

Clause 11.4: Reconnection
Supply shall be reconnected within 24 hours of payment of outstanding dues plus
reconnection charges (Rs. 50 for LT connections).

Clause 11.5: Dispute of Arrears
If a consumer disputes arrears:
  (a) A written dispute shall be filed with the Distribution Licensee;
  (b) Disconnection shall be deferred during investigation;
  (c) Investigation shall be completed within 30 days.

================================================================================
SCHEDULE 12: BILLING DISPUTE RESOLUTION PROCEDURE
================================================================================

Clause 12.1: Consumer Grievance Redressal
Any consumer aggrieved by a billing matter may:
  Step 1: Contact the local section office of the Distribution Licensee
  Step 2: If unresolved, escalate to the Division Office Consumer Grievance Cell
  Step 3: If further unresolved, file complaint with the Consumer Grievance Redressal Forum (CGRF)
  Step 4: Appeal to KERC Ombudsman

Clause 12.2: Timeline for Grievance Resolution
- Section Office: 7 working days
- Division Office CGRF: 30 days
- KERC Ombudsman: 60 days

Clause 12.3: Tariff Dispute Resolution
Where a consumer disputes the tariff category under which they are billed:
  (a) The Distribution Licensee shall verify the premises use within 30 days;
  (b) If site inspection is required, it shall be conducted within 30 days of the dispute;
  (c) A written order shall be communicated to the consumer.

PART IV: GENERAL TERMS AND CONDITIONS
================================================================================

Section 15: Overriding Effect
In case of any inconsistency between this tariff order and any other order/notification,
this tariff order shall prevail for matters related to retail tariffs.
